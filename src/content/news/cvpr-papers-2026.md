Two main papers, [Confusion-Aware Spectral Regularizer for Long-Tailed Recognition](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.16732) (**Oral**) and [Omni-Fake: Benchmarking Unified Multimodal Social Media Deepfake Detection](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.01638), along with a findings paper, [MReactor: Offline Multiple Appropriate Facial Reaction Generation with Hierarchical Cognitive Disentanglement](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2026F/html/Luo_MReactor_Offline_Multiple_Appropriate_Facial_Reaction_Generation_with_Hierarchical_Cognitive_CVPRF_2026_paper.html), have been **accepted** to [CVPR 2026](https://cvpr.thecvf.com/). Congrats to **Tianxiao (UG researcher)** and all co-authors!
