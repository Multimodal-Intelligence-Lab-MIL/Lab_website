Two main papers, [Confusion-Aware Spectral Regularizer for Long-Tailed Recognition](publications/confusion-aware-spectral-regularizer/) (**Oral**) and [Omni-Fake: Benchmarking Unified Multimodal Social Media Deepfake Detection](publications/omni-fake/), along with a findings paper, [MReactor: Offline Multiple Appropriate Facial Reaction Generation with Hierarchical Cognitive Disentanglement](publications/mreactor/), have been **accepted** to [CVPR 2026](https://cvpr.thecvf.com/). Congrats to **Tianxiao (UG researcher)** and all co-authors!
